FAQ

Common questions

Do I need experience to start?

No. The courses welcome beginners and career changers. In the free consultation we check where you are today and start from there.

How do the one-to-one sessions work?

You meet your coach live on video at times that suit you. Between sessions you work on projects and can send questions to your coach.

Are the courses fully online?

Yes. Sessions and materials are all online, so you can learn from anywhere.

What is the difference between a short course and a long program?

A short course builds one skill set. A long program covers more ground and takes longer. We will help you choose in the free consultation.

Which course should I choose?

Tell us your goal and your background. Your coach will recommend the course that fits and explain what to expect.

How much does a course cost?

Pricing depends on the course and the number of sessions you need. Book a free consultation and we will send you a clear quote.

    About us

    Tech learning that feels simple and personal.

    Simplitech Academy teaches AI, data, software, cloud and design skills through live one-to-one coaching, for learners in every time zone.

    Technology changes every month, and a recorded course starts to age the day it is published. Simplitech Academy works differently. Each learner has a coach who adapts the material to their goals, answers questions as they come up and reviews the work they build.

    We teach DevOps, cloud engineering, infrastructure and site reliability, with AI, data and design courses alongside. The name describes the approach for all of them: Simplitech means taking complex technology and explaining it clearly, one step at a time.

    Whether you are starting your first tech role or changing careers, your plan starts from where you are today.
